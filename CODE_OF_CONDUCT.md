# Code of conduct

Short, because it has to be read.

## What is expected

Be direct about the work and civil about the person. Review the change, not
the author. Assume the other side had a reason and ask what it was before
concluding they did not.

Disagreement is the point of review. "This is wrong because X" is welcome at
any volume; "you are careless" is not, at any volume.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or comments about someone's identity rather than
  their work.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, commit messages or
  privately after contact made through this project.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately wasting a reviewer's time: reopening a settled decision without
  new argument, or submitting work that has plainly not been run.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, commits, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Email **igorsvolohovs@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and not
shared further without the reporter's agreement.

The maintainer will respond within seven days with what was decided. Possible
outcomes are a warning, having a comment removed, or being blocked from the
repository.

## A note on scale

This project has one maintainer. That makes this document look
disproportionate — and it is exactly why it is written down in advance, while
nothing has happened and nobody has a stake in the answer.
